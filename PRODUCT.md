# Plog

## Users

Personal bloggers and developers who value clean content presentation over flashy design. They read long-form technical articles, bookmark resources, and reference documentation. They switch between reading and writing modes throughout the day.

## Product Purpose

A minimalist blog system with a Rust backend and static frontend. Content-first design: the writing and reading experience is the product. The admin dashboard serves the content; the public site IS the content.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, unhurried. Like a well-set typeface on quality paper. Nothing screams; everything earns its place. Technical credibility through restraint, not decoration.

## Tone

Direct and quiet. No exclamation marks in UI. No motivational copy. Labels are nouns, actions are verbs. Chinese primary.

## Anti-references

- WordPress default themes (too busy, too boxy)
- Medium (too much social chrome)
- Substack (too newsletter-centric)
- Any blog with a hero image that takes up the full viewport

## Strategic Principles

1. Content is the interface. Every pixel either serves reading or gets out.
2. Typography does the heavy lifting. Scale, weight, and spacing create hierarchy, not color or decoration.
3. Theme switching is a feature, not an afterthought. Light, dark, and sepia must all feel native.
4. Navigation is architectural. Seven pages, each with a clear purpose, no dead ends.
